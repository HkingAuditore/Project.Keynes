# Ideology V2 Art

The `collection_card_frame_*.png` files are original 1536x1024 landscape
archive-card frames generated for Project.Keynes. They are UI art only; no
source imagery from Toy Empires or another game is included.

All three frames share the hand-painted papercut language used by the ideology
workspace: warm paper grain, pencil-dark ink, oxblood book cloth, and brass
corner hardware. Their silhouettes remain distinct when the collection rail is
short.

- `collection_card_frame_v2.png`: common. Aged parchment field, oxblood spine,
  dark-green inner rail, and restrained brass corners.
- `collection_card_frame_rare_v2.png`: rare. Teal braided borders and fuller
  gilt foliate corners around the same archive-card construction.
- `collection_card_frame_legendary_v2.png`: legendary. Vermilion and black
  lacquer outer rails, dense gold cloud ornament, and a raised sun medallion.

`ideology_art.gd` maps rarity 1-3 to these textures. The card keeps its level
seal and understanding track inside the lower dark band so progression remains
visible without adding a second rarity or status caption.
