1 - Summary

Render overhead is tiny (t_render 0.02ms). Main-thread sim fast_ms mean 4.25ms / P95 6ms is well within the 8ms sim-frame budget (and 16.7ms frame budget); fps is steady at 60. The real pressure is on the native worker thread's daily simulation: its sim_slice_budget is 3.0ms, yet the rolling sus_sim mean is already 3.15ms (+5%), P95 4.74ms (+58%), max 6.85ms (+128%); native compute_ms exceeds 3ms in 33% of ticks. Optimization should target the native sim's P95/max, not the main thread.

2 - Compute distribution (native worker daily graph)

4 - Main-thread sim composition (reference only, has headroom)

Main-thread items sum to ~ fast_ms mean 4.25ms, far below the 8ms sim-frame budget - not the bottleneck; listed to locate future cuts.

5 - Optimization opportunities

  1. Ocean is the only bottleneck worth attacking (~65% of native graph, plus a separate ocean_currents job with 6.3ms spikes). The project already designed Ocean physics cell-range slicing (SLP/WIND/PSI/UPWELLING four leaf-pass slicing + GDScript cursor); it is currently design-state, disabled by default, pending local verification. Enabling and verifying it should spread the 6.3ms spikes across ticks and flatten sus_sim P95/max.
  2. PSI full sweep is the spike root cause: PSI currently does no cell-range, full Gauss-Seidel sweep. Samples show psi_iters_run=16, psi_residual_final=0.056. Introducing slicing or a higher early-exit residual threshold for PSI would sharply cut the 6.3ms peak.
  3. Wind physics inside the native-graph ocean pass (~1.09ms): if cell-range slicing covers this pass, the baseline compute (mean 2.45ms) can be pushed further toward budget, bringing steady state into compliance too.
  4. Stagger weather-pass spikes: weather active 1.7ms (duty ~25%) stacked with ocean spikes in the same tick amplifies the total peak; consider offsetting their cadences.
  5. Main-thread j_native_daily_sim shell at 1.44ms/tick is high for a "shell". Check marshalling/refresh overhead (native_call 0.27 + apply 0.33 + refresh 0.06). Main thread has headroom, but lowering this would cut fast_ms P95 (currently 6ms, max 10ms).
  6. dynamic_visual_atlas_upload 0.83ms @50% duty: visual upload boundary, can be batched/throttled to lower duty.

6 - Quantified optimization potential

  • If ocean_currents 6.3ms spikes are spread to <=3ms via slicing: sus_sim max 6.85->~3.5ms, P95 4.74->~3.3ms, back near budget.
  • If native-graph ocean pass drops 30% (slicing + PSI early-exit): baseline compute 2.45->~2.0ms, steady-state compliant, ticks over 3ms 33%->near 0.
  • Just these two Ocean items turn "over budget" from chronic (mean already over) to occasional, without touching fps (already 60).

Method

Timing fields contain a -1 sentinel (tick did not run), dropped as missing. Periodic jobs report both active-mean (running ticks only) and duty (running ticks / total ticks) plus amortized value. "Native graph" = the native_daily_graph shared by climate/weather bundles. All numbers derived directly from the 327 sampled rows.
